Nonlinearity of the Aerodynamic Characteristics of
NACA0012 Aerofoil at Low Reynolds Numbers
Bg C] CR 5W∗2!&Cf 9> M: N<∗3!&K\ 66 N6 O:∗2
Tomohisa Ohtake, Yusuke Nakae and Tatsuo Motohashi
Key W ords : Low Reynolds Numbers, Nonlinearity, Aerodynamic Characteristics, NACA0012
Abstract : Aerodynamic characteristics of NACA0012 airfoil at low Reynolds numbers ( Re = 1 .0 × 104!A1.0 × 105)
are measured systematically to clarify nonlinearity of the aerofoil characteristics. The variation of the lift curve with
the angle of attack is divided into 5 sub-regions; the gradient of the lift curve much depends on the incident angle.
Negative values of the gradient of lift coeﬃcient are observed at lower angles of attack in a Reynolds numbers range.
The sub-regions are summarized in a diagram of angle of attack and Reynolds numbers. The coeﬃcients of drag and
moment around 0.25 chord length have also unique characteristics.
